Doomsday Tunnels 3D · Support

Help for pilots

Doomsday Tunnels 3D is a tunnel-flying arcade game for iPhone. Each sector has a core counting down to detonation: fly to the far end before it hits zero, dodging the red walls, shooting the yellow ones and collecting the white cells on the way.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or have a question this page does not answer? Open an issue on GitHub. Say which iPhone you have and what you were doing when it happened. Every report is read.

How to play

  • Steer with your left thumb, anywhere on the left of the screen. Fire with your right thumb. The boost button gives you speed while its fuel lasts.
  • Red walls take your shield when you hit them. Dodge them.
  • Yellow walls break when you shoot them. The guns overheat if you hold the trigger too long.
  • White cells top up your shield, your boost or your clock, and a ship token earns a spare ship.
  • Losing your shield or running out of time costs a ship. The run ends when the last one goes.
  • Some sectors fork. Either branch reaches the exit.

Questions

Can I steer by tilting the phone?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the title screen, then Tilt phone under Steering. Tilt then steers and your left thumb aims the guns. Tilt re-centres to however you are holding the phone each time a sector starts or the game resumes. Invert tilt up/down is there too.

The steering feels too fast or too slow.

The same gear button opens seven feel sliders, for steering speed, snap, flight speed and more. Reset sliders puts them back. Sector times only count for the records at the standard flight speed.

Why can I hear the game with the silent switch on?

The game plays through the silent switch, like a music app. The speaker button at the top right of the screens between runs turns all sound off. Music and sound effects can also be switched separately in the settings.

What is the daily sector?

A new sector every day, the same for everyone. Tap Daily on the title screen, fly it as many times as you like, and post your best time to that day's board.

What is the faint ship flying beside me?

Your ghost: a replay of your fastest run through that sector. Beat it and the new run becomes the ghost. You can turn it off in the settings.

How do I get on the scoreboard?

When a run ends, tap Post score and type a name of up to 12 characters. Posting is always optional and needs a connection. A name is not an account, so anyone can type any name. To have a name removed, open an issue on GitHub.

What does the supporter purchase do?

The game is free and nothing is locked. After a game it may ask you to support its development with a one-time purchase. Buying it stops that request for good, and a thank-you appears on the credits card. It is the only purchase in the game.

I bought it, but the request came back on a new phone.

On the request card, or under Credits then Support, tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account. The game also checks with Apple when it starts, so it usually restores itself once the phone is online.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which phones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 15 or later. It plays in landscape only. It also installs on iPad, where it runs as an iPhone app.
