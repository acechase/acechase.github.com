The ship you are flying is a rental

Prestige Rocket Rental vehicle, light grey with a white stripe down the spine, red wings and tail, yellow nozzles. Seen here from the nose, the way the Tunnels of Armageddon manual drew its own. Every figure on this sheet is the number the flight model actually uses.

Vehicle data plate Prestige Rocket Rental · mileage plan: Earth

Cruise20 m/s in sector 1, 2 m/s more each sector, 38 m/s at most. Boost multiplies by 1.5.
Boost reserve6 s with the button held; refilled by a boost pickup.
GunsShots fly 95 m/s faster than the ship, 30 m ahead. 18 in a row overheat the guns; they return once cooled to 30 percent.
Shield100 units. Red wall 20, gate block 18, mine 16, fork stub 18, moving hazard 16, scraping 11 a second. A shield pickup is 25; a cleared sector refills 20.
Spare shipsNone to start, five at most. One waits at the end of any sector whose ship token you grabbed (it is always down the dark branch).
HitboxNose 0.42 m ahead of centre, wings 0.24 m to each side, hull 0.10 m above and below. A fresh ship is untouchable for 3 s.
Off a red wallHeld just short of it, thrown back at 5 m/s or 30 percent of your speed, and shoved toward the nearest gap, far enough to clear it and no further.
WarpClimbs to 420 m/s over 1.4 s; white-out at 1.5 s; the clear card at 2.6 s.
Return with shield at received level

Debbie at Prestige Rocket Rental would like it noted that the vehicle has left the planet, which is a different mileage plan, and that in the event the pilot does not make it back, the company will be billing the estate.
